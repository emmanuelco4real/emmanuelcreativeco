Brand Identity Design

Gourguex Real Estate Brand Identity

A sophisticated brand identity system for a luxury real estate company, designed to communicate elegance, trust, and exclusivity in the competitive property market.

3 Weeks Duration
Brand Identity Category
Complete Status

Project Overview

Gourguex is a premium real estate brand specializing in luxury properties and exclusive developments. They needed a complete brand identity that would position them as a trusted, sophisticated leader in the high-end real estate market.

Challenge

Gourguex aimed to create an elegant trusted Real Estate brand but struggled with inconsistency in its branding. The company needed a strong brand identity that communicated Elegance, Sophistication, Luxury, and reliability to clients.

Solution

The designed logo features a Sophisticated yet professional mark, symbolizing Elegance. The Green and Golden reflects Elegance, Luxury, and Sophistication, making the brand approachable. A full 35-page brand guidelines ensures consistent brand communication across all platforms, and advertising. This structured branding approach allows Gourguex to build a strong emotional connection with its Clients.
