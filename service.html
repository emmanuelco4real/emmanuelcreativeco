Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services, process, and pricing.

How long does a typical project take?

+

Project timelines vary depending on the scope and complexity. A basic logo design might take 1-2 weeks, while a complete brand identity with website could take 4-6 weeks. We provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.

Do you offer revisions?

+

Yes, we include a specific number of revision rounds in each package. The number varies by package: Basic includes 2 rounds, Standard includes 4, and Premium includes unlimited revisions until you're completely satisfied.

What if I need additional services later?

+

We're happy to continue working with you as your business grows. Existing clients receive priority scheduling and a 15% discount on additional services requested within 6 months of project completion.

How do we get started?

+

Getting started is easy! Simply contact us through the form below or email us directly. We'll schedule a free consultation to discuss your project, goals, and requirements before providing a detailed quote.

What forms of payment do you accept?

+

We accept bank transfers, and online payments. Projects are typically broken into milestones with payments due at each stage. We require a 50% deposit to begin work, with the balance due upon project completion.

Will I own the final designs?

+

Yes, once the final payment is made, you will own all rights to the final designs. We provide all source files and documentation. We only retain the right to display the work in our portfolio unless otherwise agreed.
