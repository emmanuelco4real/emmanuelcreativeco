Brand Identity Design

Velour Luxe Perfume Brand Identity

A sophisticated brand identity for a luxury perfume house, designed to evoke elegance, exclusivity, and sensory delight through refined visual elements and premium aesthetics.

4 Weeks Duration
Luxury Branding Category
Complete Status

Project Overview

Velour Luxe is a premium fragrance brand specializing in exclusive, handcrafted perfumes for discerning clients. They needed a complete brand identity that would position them as an elite, sophisticated choice in the competitive luxury fragrance market.

Challenge

Velour Luxe aimed to establish itself as a premium fragrance house but struggled with an inconsistent brand identity. The company needed a sophisticated visual system that communicated elegance, exclusivity, and sensory luxury to differentiate from mass-market perfumes.

Solution

The redesigned logo features an elegant monogram forming a subtle perfume bottle silhouette, symbolizing sophistication and luxury. The deep burgundy color palette evokes richness and exclusivity, while refined serif typography enhances the premium feel. A comprehensive brand guidelines system ensures consistent application across all touchpoints, from packaging to digital presence, creating a cohesive luxury experience that resonates with high-end clientele.
