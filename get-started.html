Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A simple logo design might take 1-2 weeks, while a comprehensive brand identity could take 3-5 weeks. I'll provide a detailed timeline after our initial consultation.

What is your revision policy?

Each package includes a specific number of revision rounds. I want you to be completely satisfied with the final design, so I'm committed to working with you until we get it right. Additional revisions beyond the included number can be arranged at an hourly rate.

How do we communicate during the project?

I prefer to use a combination of email for formal communication and either Zoom or Google Meet for video calls when needed. I'm also available on WhatsApp for quick questions. We'll establish our preferred communication channels at the start of the project.

What forms of payment do you accept?

I accept bank transfers, PayPal, and other digital payment methods. A 50% deposit is required to begin work, with the balance due upon project completion.

Will I own the rights to the final design?

Yes, once the final payment is received, you will own all rights to the final design files. I only retain the right to display the work in my portfolio (unless otherwise agreed upon).

What if I need additional services after project completion?

I offer ongoing support and design services for existing clients. Whether you need minor adjustments down the line or additional design work, I'm happy to help. Returning clients receive priority scheduling and discounted rates.
