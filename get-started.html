Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical project take?

The timeline varies depending on the project scope and complexity. A basic logo design might take 1-2 weeks, while a complete brand identity system could take 3-4 weeks. Website design projects typically take 4-6 weeks. I'll provide a detailed timeline after our initial discussion.

What's included in your branding packages?

My branding packages include logo design, color palette, typography system, and brand guidelines. More comprehensive packages include additional items like business cards, letterheads, social media assets, and style guides. All packages include multiple concepts and revision rounds.

How do revisions work?

Each package includes a specific number of revision rounds. After I present the initial concepts, you'll provide feedback, and I'll refine the selected concept. We'll continue this process until you're completely satisfied with the result (within the agreed number of revisions).

What file formats will I receive?

You'll receive all final designs in the appropriate formats for both print and digital use. This typically includes high-resolution PDF, PNG, JPEG, and vector files (AI, EPS, SVG). For web projects, you'll receive optimized image files and any necessary code files.

Do you offer ongoing support after project completion?

Yes, I offer ongoing support packages for clients who need regular design work or occasional updates. I also provide a 30-day period of complimentary support for any minor adjustments needed after project delivery.
