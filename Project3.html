Brand Identity Design

Pawma Pet's Brand Identity

A vibrant and playful brand identity for a pet care company, designed to communicate warmth, care, and reliability to pet owners while maintaining a professional appearance.

6 Weeks Duration
Brand Identity Category
Complete Status

Project Overview

Pawma is a pet care brand specializing in premium pet products and services. They needed a complete brand identity that would appeal to pet owners while communicating trust, care, and quality for their furry family members.

Challenge

Pawma aimed to create a trusted pet brand but struggled with inconsistency in its branding. The company needed a strong brand identity that communicated warmth, care, fun, and reliability to pet owners while standing out in the competitive pet care market.

Solution

The designed logo features a playful yet professional mark—a stylized "P" where the negative space forms a silhouette of both a dog and a cat, symbolizing dedication to providing care and products for all beloved pets. The vibrant golden-yellow color reflects warmth, friendliness, and joy, making the brand approachable. A full 30-page brand guideline ensures consistent brand communication across packaging, digital presence, and advertising. This structured branding approach allows Pawma to build a strong emotional connection with pet owners.
